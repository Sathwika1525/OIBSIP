About Ratan Tata

Ratan Naval Tata is one of India’s most respected industrialists and philanthropists. He served as the Chairman of Tata Group from 1991 to 2012 and played a key role in turning Tata into a global business giant. Under his leadership, Tata Group acquired famous international brands like Jaguar, Land Rover, and Tetley.

Major Achievements

  • Former Chairman of Tata Group
  • Introduced Tata Nano – the world’s most affordable car
  • Expanded Tata Group globally
  • Recipient of Padma Bhushan and Padma Vibhushan
